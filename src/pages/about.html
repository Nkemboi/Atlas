Who we are

The desk on Thika Road, Exit 7.

Atlas Auto Service Centre opened in 2020 as an Isuzu East Africa authorised service outlet — opposite Garden City Mall, on the route for Runda, Karen, Muthaiga and Ridgeways.

Nine bays since day one: diagnostics, genuine parts, fleet support, recovery, alignment, body and paint, detailing, tracking and governors, electrical and A/C. Personal vehicles and fleet units book through the same desk and are held to the same sheet.

The standard is simple: diagnose before you quote, quote in writing, and only then put a spanner to the vehicle. It applies to the director's SUV and to the fleet unit that must be back on the road by Monday.
