Fleet Management Support

A fleet is a promise to someone else's schedule. You get a single point of contact, priority bays, and reporting that reads well in a Monday meeting.

What this bay does

Fleet Support

Priority scheduling and reporting for the teams that report to you.

  • One point of contact for your transport or operations lead
  • Priority scheduling and agreed turnaround windows
  • Per-unit service history and condition reporting
  • Consolidated invoicing and maintenance planning
  • Staff personal vehicles handled to the same standard
How it works

The same process, every bay.

However the vehicle arrives — booked, referred or recovered — it moves through the same five steps.

01

Book the desk

Call, WhatsApp or the booking form. You get a slot and a named contact — not a queue.

02

Diagnose & inspect

Computer diagnostics first, then a physical inspection on the ramp. Findings before opinions.

03

Written estimate

A clear quote by call or WhatsApp. Nothing is touched until you approve it.

04

Work & quality check

The bay completes the work to factory standards, then checks it before handover.

05

Handover & history

You collect the vehicle with a record of what was done — useful for the next service, the resale, or the board.
